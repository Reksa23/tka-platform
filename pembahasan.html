Tempe Buatan Rumah

Tempeh, a fermented soybean cake originally from Java, is often praised abroad as a “superfood,” yet fewer people know how straightforward it is to make at home with just a few ingredients and a warm, humid spot in the kitchen. Making tempeh yourself not only costs far less than buying it packaged but also lets you control exactly how firm or how mild the finished cake turns out to be.

To begin, you will need half a kilogram of dried soybeans, a tablespoon of tempeh starter culture known as ragi tempe, a pot large enough to boil the beans, a colander for draining, and either small perforated plastic bags or fresh banana leaves for wrapping. A needle or a thin skewer is also useful for poking tiny air holes into the wrapping material.

First, soak the dried soybeans in waterTahap 1: rendam dan kupasKata First menandai awal proses. for about eight hours or overnight, then rub the softened beans between your hands to loosen and remove their outer skins, which should float to the surface and can be skimmed away. Next, boil the peeled beansTahap 2: rebus dan dinginkanDirebus setelah dikupas, lalu didinginkan sampai hangat-hangat kuku. for thirty to forty minutes until they are tender but not mushy, then drain them thoroughly and spread them out to cool until they feel barely warm to the touch.

Once the beans have cooled, sprinkle the ragi tempe evenly over themTahap 3: campur ragiRagi baru ditabur Once the beans have cooled, yaitu setelah kacang dingin. and mix well so that every bean is lightly coated with the starter culture. Pack the inoculated beans tightly into the prepared bags or banana leaf parcels, pressing out any trapped air, then use the needle to poke small holes every two to three centimetresTahap 4: bungkus dan lubangiKacang dibungkus rapat, lalu dilubangi agar jamur bisa bernapas. along each parcel so the growing mould can breathe.

Leave the wrapped parcels undisturbedTahap 5: fermentasiTahap terakhir: diamkan di tempat hangat 24 sampai 36 jam. in a warm spot, ideally around thirty degrees Celsius, for twenty-four to thirty-six hours, checking occasionally for a thin layer of white mould binding the beans into a firm block. The tempeh is ready once it holds together as a solid cake, but if you notice a strong ammonia-like smell or patches of black or grey mould rather than white, it is a sign that fermentation has gone wrong and the batch should be discardedJawaban soal 6Bau amonia kuat berarti fermentasi gagal, dan teks menyuruh adonan dibuang..

Wae Rebo

Hidden in the highlands of Flores, East Nusa Tenggara, Wae Rebo sits more than a thousand metres above sea levelTersurat (soal 8, B)Ketinggian disebut langsung di kalimat pertama., wrapped in mist for most of the year. Reaching the village requires a three-to-four-hour trek through dense forest, a journey that keeps Wae Rebo largely untouched by modern developmentPetunjuk soal 9Perhatikan kata largely (sebagian besar). Artinya belum banyak berubah, bukan menolak total. and gives visitors the feeling of stepping into another era.

The village is best known for its seven cone-shaped houses called Mbaru Niang, each rising nearly fifteen metres and built entirely from wood, bamboo, and layers of dried palm fibre without a single nailTersurat (soal 8, D)Rumah Mbaru Niang dibangun tanpa paku, ditulis langsung di teks.. Inside, the houses are divided into five levels, with the lowest floor used for daily family life and the upper levels reserved for storing seeds, food supplies, and, at the very top, offerings for the ancestors. Around a hundred people, all descended from the same ancestor, still live according to customs passed down for generations. Coffee farming remains their main source of income, and visitors are often invited to help pick and roast beans grown on the surrounding slopes, an experience that has slowly made Wae Rebo known among travellers seeking something beyond typical tourist destinations.

What makes the village remarkable is not only its architecture but also the quiet rhythm of life within it. There are no motor vehicles, no shops selling souvenirs, and electricity reached the village only within the last decadeTersurat (soal 8, E)Within the last decade berarti dalam sepuluh tahun terakhir., through a small hydroelectric generator. In 2012, Wae Rebo received the UNESCO Asia-Pacific Award for Cultural Heritage Conservation, recognising the community's effort to preserve both its unique houses and its way of life.

Tambang Laut Dalam

As the world races to replace fossil fuels with batteries for electric vehicles and renewable energy storage, demand for minerals such as cobalt, nickel, and manganese has surged, and mining companies have begun eyeing an unexpected source: the deep ocean floor. Vast fields of potatosized rock nodules containing these metals lie scattered across the seabed, thousands of metres below the surface, and while extracting them could speed up the shift away from fossil fuels, deep-sea mining should not go ahead until its long-term effects are far better understood. Mining companies argue that seabed nodules can be collected without the extensive land clearing, ore refining waste, and community displacementArgumen mendukung (soal 11)Ini klaim perusahaan tambang: pengambilan di dasar laut disebut lebih bersih daripada tambang darat. typically associated with land-based mining. A single field surveyed in the Pacific Ocean is estimated to hold more nickel and cobalt than all known reserves on land combinedArgumen mendukung (soal 11)Jumlah yang besar dipakai sebagai alasan untuk menambang., offering a potential shortcut past the supply bottlenecks currently slowing battery production worldwide.

Marine biologists, however, warn that the deep seabed hosts ecosystems scientists have barely begun to studyArgumen menentangPembicaranya para ahli biologi laut. Semua pilihan soal 11 yang berasal dari sini bukan argumen pendukung., with some species discovered only in the last decade found nowhere else on Earth. Mining equipment dragged across the ocean floor would stir up sediment clouds capable of smothering marine life far beyond the immediate mining site, and because deep-sea organisms often grow and reproduce extremely slowly, any damage caused could take centuries, rather than years, to recover.

Weighing these two positions together suggests a middle pathKunci soal 12 dan 13Penulis tidak melarang total dan tidak menyetujui penuh. Ia memilih jalan tengah.: rather than banning or approving deep-sea mining outright, regulators could support strict independent environmental monitoring and limit extraction to carefully mapped zones while more research is carried out, allowing the mineral supply chain to grow without repeating the irreversible mistakes made in some land-based mining regions.

For this reason, permitting large-scale deep-sea mining before its ecological consequences are fully understood carries a risk too significant to ignore. A slower, evidence-based approachSikap penulis (soal 13)Penulis condong ke riset dan kehati-hatian sebelum penambangan besar-besaran. would allow the world to secure the minerals it needs for clean energy without causing damage to ecosystems that, once destroyed, may never recover.

The Challenges of Distance Learning

Distance learning is a method to study without having to gather in a physical classroom. Students learn through online platforms, using live broadcasts or pre-recorded lessons. This method takes advantage of the internet to provide flexibility in accessing education. However, distance learning comes with some disadvantages.

First, it lacks the face-to-face interaction found in physical classrooms. Students can only communicate through chats or video platforms, which can limit social engagement and the ability to build strong relationships with peers.

Second, it’s not suitable for every learning style. Students who rely on frequent motivation, feedback, or close support from instructors may struggle, as teachers are not always available in real timeInti paragraf 3 (soal 19)Masalahnya ada pada dukungan instruktur yang tidak selalu tersedia saat itu juga..

Another drawback is that not all required courses for a degree may be offered online. In some cases, universities may offer only a portion of the curriculum virtually, and students must attend some classes in person. This can be inconvenient or expensive, especially for those who live far from campus.

Additionally, distance learning requires digital literacy. Students must be able to use online tools, participate in virtual discussions, and take online exams. This may not be a problem for students who are digital savvyPetunjuk soal 17Kalimat sebelumnya menyebut digital literacy. Setelah However, kelompok yang kontras adalah mereka yang tidak nyaman dengan teknologi.. However, those uncomfortable with technology or who prefer printed materials may find it difficult to adjust.

In summary, while distance learning offers flexibility and broader access to education, it also presents challenges such as limited social interaction, learning support, course availability, and the need for tech skills. This relatively new style of studying still needs a lot of improvement to address these issues.

Auroras

Auroras are glowing lights in the night sky that appear mostly near the Earth’s polar regionsJawaban soal 24Lokasi utama aurora: dekat wilayah kutub.. Near the North Pole, they’re known as the Northern Lights; near the South Pole, the Southern Lights. These lights often appear as arcs, curtains, or waves that shimmer and shift across the sky.

Though caused by the sun, auroras are not sunlightKontras (soal 26)Dua hal berlawanan: dihasilkan oleh matahari, tetapi bukan sinar matahari. Itu sebabnya konjungsi kontras yang dipakai.. The sun releases a stream of charged particles known as the solar wind, which travels rapidly through space toward Earth. When these particles reach Earth, they interact with the planet’s magnetic field, which directs them toward the polar areasPetunjuk soal 25Medan magnet Bumi mengarahkan partikel ke kutub.. This process is more active during periods of strong solar activity, such as solar flares.

As the solar wind enters the magnetosphere, it moves into the Van Allen radiation belt and is guided toward the magnetic poles. There, the particles collide with gases in the atmospherePenyebab aurora (soal 25)Tumbukan partikel Matahari dengan gas atmosfer menghasilkan cahaya., which are mostly oxygen and nitrogen. The collisions create glowing colors that form the aurora. The colors vary depending on the type of gas and the altitude of the collision. Oxygen tends to produce green or red light, while nitrogen can give off blue or purple hues.

Auroras are usually only visible at nightSoal 27Usually berarti biasanya. Cari kata lain yang bermakna “pada umumnya”., because the light they emit is not bright enough to be seen during the day. They are best seen in dark, clear skies far from city lights, and are most common in regions like Alaska, Norway, and New Zealand.
